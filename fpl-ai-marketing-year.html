01The idea in one line

Launch inside the current season, small and soon — then spend eleven months turning the bot's public results into a marketing compound that pays off everything at GW1 of the next season. The product does the talking: every gameweek the AI team agent publishes what it did, why, and what it scored.

05The contest machine

Prizes make the community play with us, not just read us. Everything runs off one mechanic: a single mini-league for the whole community — one table, every language on it, the same prizes — with only the announcements translated. Real prizes every month, and the grand prize timed to the season-two launch.

CompetitionWho entersPrizesWhen
Launch leaguefree entry Anyone with an account — the waitlist's first reason to sign up Top 3: €40 / €20 / €10 gift cards — one table, three winners, all languages together Launch week → end of 2026
Monthly pots Runs continuously; monthly reset on the 1st €40 / €20 / €10 per month on the one league table — three winners a month, language-agnostic Nov 2026 → Apr 2027
Beat the botAny paid member who outscores the bot's public team in a GW Weekly draw for a €10 prize; winners' names on the founding page Every GW from launch
Season grand prizeThe same league table, running all season €150 + a club shirt of their choice + founding price locked for life Decided May 2027, presented in the Season Report
  • One table on purpose. English, French and Arabic managers compete for the same prizes on the same standings — the multilingual community is the product, and the league proves it every GW. Winners are announced in all three languages; nothing is ever split by language.
  • Prizes are marketing spend, not a cost. Every winner posts their result — three language communities each month producing proof and screenshots on our behalf.
  • Contest content is the receipt's sibling: "our league's GW12 champion did X with the agent's calls" is a testimonial with a scoreboard.
  • Run it clean: published rules on site, independent verification (the official FPL league table is the scoreboard), winners announced in all three languages, no purchase necessary for the launch and monthly leagues — only the members-only extras require an active subscription.

Contest winners and the monthly pots are announced in the same posts, so the contest machine rides the same engine for free.

07Channels, honestly ranked

  • X / Twitter FPL community — fastest to reach, most price-sensitive to results. Primary channel for the weekly engine.
  • Reddit (r/FantasyPL) — biggest audience, strictest on self-promotion. Engage first, link only when the weekly receipt is genuinely useful; play the long game.
  • WhatsApp & Telegram groups — where the Arabic-speaking FPL community actually lives. One good MENA Telegram share beats a week of Facebook-style posting; get into groups by being useful, and give the group admins a free season.
  • Free tools as SEO — price ticker, captain matrix, squad builder. Tools get searched in July like clockwork; content pages don't have to. This is the compounding channel.
  • TikTok / Shorts — screen-record the agent reasoning ("it's selling Palmer, here's why — he's one knock from rotation"). Cheap to make, big reach, low intent — awareness only.
  • Email — the only channel you own. Deadline-eve every gameweek from October.
  • Creators (affiliates) — buy attention only in the two pre-paid peaks (Feb, Jul–Aug). Pay per verified conversion, not flat, until one creator proves flat-fee is worth it.
  • Discord — retention and testimonials, not acquisition. Founding members' private channel from day one.

08Budget for the year

  • €0–300  — launch window: nothing paid, all earned, maybe €150 Reddit/X promo in December only
  • €300–600 — Jan–Feb creator placements, pay-per-conversion where possible
  • €400–800 — Jul–Aug 2027 paid push, concentrated GW−2 to GW+2
  • ~€900    — the contest machine's prize pool across the year (one league table: ~€70/mo × 9 monthly pots + launch league + the €150 grand prize) — paid back in community proof
  • €700–1,000 — infra + email + LLM across the year (grows with, not ahead of, paying users)

Rule: every pound of promo is spent pointed at a deadline (founding-price close, price rise, GW1). Deadlines convert; ambient ads don't.

09What could kill it

  • Launching late. In-season launch is the whole point — projections during a live season make subscribers; a launch at GW20 is a hard sell. The 4-week sprint is a deadline, not a wish.
  • Missing the receipt. One week of "we forgot to post the results" and the trust engine stalls. Automate it before you automate anything else.
  • Spamming Reddit. One self-promotion ban and the main organic channel is gone. Community karma first, links later.
  • A bad month, hidden. Post the misses. A visible bot that recovers is more convincing than a perfect one.
